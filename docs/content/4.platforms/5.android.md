---
title: Android
description: Android keystore storage through android-native-keyring-store; no setup needed.
---

Entries are stored by `android-native-keyring-store`: secrets are encrypted with a key held in the
**Android Keystore** and kept in the app's **SharedPreferences**. They are private to your app.

- **Setup:** none. The store is created from the activity context that Tauri (tao) registers with
  `ndk-context`, on the first call.
- **Prompts:** none.
- **Uninstall:** Android deletes the app's data, so the entries are gone. Use an
  [encrypted backup](/guides/passwords#encrypted) if users must keep secrets across reinstalls.
- **Backups of the device:** the Keystore key does not leave the device, so restored SharedPreferences
  on a new device cannot be decrypted.

The plugin's CI cross-builds the crate for `aarch64-linux-android`.
